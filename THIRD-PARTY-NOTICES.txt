--------------

Project URL:   https://github.com/empira/PDFsharp

The managed runtime assemblies of PDFsharp 6.2.4 are compiled into the GUI
executable of this product (self-contained single-file distribution). PDFsharp
is used exclusively to read PDF metadata (page count and page sizes) for output
validation in the GUI process. It never participates in the NX export itself.

Bundled character illustrations
-------------------------------
The four presets contain AI-generated fan illustrations of characters from
Re:Zero, Guilty Crown, Frieren: Beyond Journey's End, and Mushoku Tensei.
Character and franchise rights remain with their respective rights holders.
See assets/themes/SOURCES.txt in the portable package, or
src/NxDrawingPdfExporter.App/Assets/Themes/SOURCES.txt in the source tree, for
character names, generation details, and provenance.
